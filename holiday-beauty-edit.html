The Holiday Beauty Edit: Get Ready With What You Already Own

By Lexi · Beauty

Before you buy anything for the party, open your makeup bag. Put on the lipstick you already know you love, then see what the look actually needs. Most nights, I stop right there.

Holiday beauty can make a perfectly good makeup bag feel as though it’s missing something. I know the feeling. A sparkly palette looks especially convincing when there’s a dinner on the calendar and you still haven’t decided what to wear. But the look I come back to is usually made up of things I’ve nearly used up. I know how they sit on my skin, how they look in the hallway mirror, and which one I’ll actually remember to take with me.

Start with what feels good on your skin

Use the moisturizer you already reach for and give it a minute to settle while you get dressed. If you wear foundation or skin tint, start with a thin layer where you want it. If you’d rather just use concealer, dab a little around your nose or wherever you’d like some coverage. I like to leave my cheeks alone if I’m happy with how they look. It saves time and makes it easier to see how much blush I actually want.

Pick one thing to dress up

For me, it’s usually a berry lip. I put it on early, then see whether I want mascara, blush, or both. If the color feels a little bold, I blot it once with a tissue. The same lipstick feels softer, and I can touch it up later without a mirror if I’ve blotted it down to a stain.

If you’d rather play up your eyes, try the brown shadow you already wear. Press a little along the upper lash line with a small brush, then soften the edge. Add mascara and whatever lip color you’d normally wear out the door. You can get a whole evening look out of the two shades you’ve already hit pan on.

Keep a five-minute version

When someone texts I’m outside and you’re still in slippers, here’s where I’d spend the time.

  1. Dab on concealer where you want it.
  2. Add a little blush and blend it with your fingertips or your usual brush.
  3. Brush on mascara, then put on your lipstick or tinted balm.
  4. Give yourself one look in the mirror by the door, grab the lip color, and go.

If you have a minute earlier in the day, set out the few things you plan to use. I put my earrings next to my lipstick so I remember both. It’s a small favor to do for yourself when dinner’s at seven and you’re still looking for the other shoe at six forty-five.

My favorite part of getting ready this way is that I can spend less time thinking about my makeup once I’m there. I can eat dinner, refill someone’s glass, and enjoy being out. If the lipstick wears off by dessert, that’s usually a sign I had a good time.
