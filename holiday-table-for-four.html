A Holiday Table for Four, Set With What You Have

By Lexi · Lifestyle

Four people are coming for dinner. Before you buy anything for the table, pull out four plates and see what you already have.

I start with the plates I use every day. If I only have two of one kind and two of another, I put matching plates across from each other. Add drinking glasses and a folded napkin at each seat, and the table already looks like you meant to set it that way.

Give each guest a place

Center the plate in front of the chair. Put the fork to the left, the knife to the right with its blade facing the plate, and the glass just above the knife. Set a cloth napkin on the plate or tuck it under the fork. A clean tea towel folded into a rectangle can stand in for a cloth napkin.

Keep the center low

I like a few evergreen sprigs in a short bowl. A small bowl of oranges works just as well, especially if you already have them in the kitchen. Sit down at one of the places when you’re finished. If you can see the person across from you and there’s room to put down a serving dish, the centerpiece has done its job.

Set it before the kitchen gets busy

  1. An hour before dinner. Set the plates, napkins, glasses, and flatware.
  2. Twenty minutes before. Put out the centerpiece and a trivet or two. Make room for whatever you’re serving at the table.
  3. Just before you sit down. Fill the water glasses and bring the food out.

Once everyone sits down, the table will fill up with serving spoons, glasses, and hands reaching for the good stuff. I like to leave some room for that.
